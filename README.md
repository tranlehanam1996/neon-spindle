# Doorways Worth Noticing

*A compact notebook about old doorways, worn thresholds, and the architectural details that give a street its character.*

A doorway compresses a building's history into a small frame of stone, timber, metal, glass, and repeated touch.

## Three things to notice

- A polished brass plate records where generations of hands have pushed.
- Fanlights borrow daylight for narrow halls while preserving privacy below.
- Deep thresholds reveal walls built for weather, weight, and a different pace of construction.

Looking at entrances turns an ordinary block into a gallery of proportion, material, and local habit.
